About

I believe in creating objects where function and aesthetics meet.

I’m a product designer with a Master’s in Industrial and Product Design from the Faculty of Fine Arts of the University of Porto, in collaboration with the Faculty of Engineering of the University of Porto.

My practice starts with people: I observe, research, prototype and test in real contexts, involving users from the earliest stages. I believe good design comes from methodological rigour, attention to detail and an understanding of materials and processes, always looking for solutions that are functional, inclusive and meaningful.

I want to work with a dynamic team, contribute to the development of innovative, user-centred products, and keep growing as a designer.

Design for Health

AID

Group project from the second year of my Bachelor’s. The challenge was to rethink a cochlear hearing device and make it more attractive and comfortable, so the user can move freely, even during intense physical activity, without the risk of it falling off.

Several research phases led to an aesthetic proposal that combines inclusion and identity.

Our professor’s guidance shaped the process and my approach to the discipline. The project brought technical learning and personal growth, and I came to see design as a vehicle for social impact.

Year2023·TypeMid-term, 1.5 months·FormatGroup

Sente & Conta

An intergenerational sensory game designed to create opportunities for connection and memory sharing among people living with dementia.

Developed as my Master’s thesis during an internship at 4Humanz, the game brings together tactile, visual, olfactory and auditory stimuli. A cork die carries familiar images, an octahedral die releases aromatic scents, and a sound-trigger button adds another layer of sensory exploration. The components invite open-ended handling, conversation and, when desired, the sharing of personal memories.

The outcome explores how playful, sensory objects can create moments of interaction across generations, not by testing memory, but by giving memories a reason to surface and be shared.

Year2025–2026·TypeMaster’s thesis, at 4Humanz·FormatIndividual · Participatory design

Industrial Design

Umbra

A cutlery set designed for the company Herdmar. It takes the angular, monumental forms of Gothic architecture and reinterprets them in a minimalist style.

The contrast between the two aesthetics gives the set a sophisticated, striking tone. The silhouettes recall pointed arches and pinnacles, without the ornamental excess of traditional Gothic design.

Year2024·TypeMid-term, 2 months·FormatIndividual

Curves

The brief was an electric grill tailored to Generation Z. Curves is a multifunctional object that grills, carries kitchen utensils, stores accessories and turns its lid into a side table.

Portability, practicality and durability guided the design, with local, high-quality materials and the user experience at the centre.

With a more critical eye for materials today, I would rethink the bamboo board, which sits in an area exposed to high temperatures, and replace it with a safer solution.

Year2022·TypeMid-term, 2 months·FormatGroup

Amov

A tribute to the forms of atomic physics. With dynamic lines that capture the movement and energy of electrons, the Amov candelabrum is a functional object and a piece that balances science and aesthetics within a space.

Year2022·TypeShort-term, 5 days·FormatIndividual · Self-initiated
03

Packaging Design

Passion Cube

In a day and a half, I created a premium packaging concept for a passion fruit and rosemary drink (4% alcohol), designed for an audience that values elegance and freshness.

Starting from a moodboard and a comparative analysis, I developed a glass jar with a metallic lid and a subtle, smoke-inspired label, illustrated with the drink’s ingredients and evoking the atmosphere of summer festivals.

Working alone under a strict time limit strengthened my independence and showed me the full range of my technical skills.

Year2025·TypeShort-term, 1.5 days·FormatIndividual · Self-initiated

Chabuire

It started from a quick sketch, then developed through AI-assisted iteration toward a final render. The goal was to learn how to communicate effectively with AI tools and to observe how small instructions can significantly change the result.

It showed me that mastering AI matters both as support for the creative process and as a way to stay competitive in the design market.

Year2025·TypeShort-term, 1.5 days·FormatIndividual · Self-initiated
04

Handmade

50 Spoons

Carried out at ABADIR Academy in Sicily during my Erasmus+ exchange, on the theme “Mediterranean”. The spoon is a utensil shared across the whole Mediterranean basin, its function intertwined with the histories and cultures of its peoples.

From the geometry of architecture to marine life, each collection celebrates a distinct element of Mediterranean heritage. The brief was to design and produce 50 wooden spoons, all carved by hand with a colleague.

It immersed me in manual craftsmanship and helped me understand the relationship between design, culture and tradition.

Year2024·TypeLong-term, 3 months·FormatGroup
05

Biomaterials

Tortionis

Made during “Diseñar para fabricar con biomateriales: del material al prototipo”, a hands-on bootcamp at the Basque Biodesign Center exploring material-to-prototype workflows. Tortionis is a lamp shaped by hand-felting Latxa sheep wool, its silhouette drawn from the organic torsions found in nature.

In six hours, we went from exploring the material to a working prototype. The process showed me how a local biomaterial can give an everyday object its form, structure and atmosphere.

YearJul 2026·TypeBootcamp, 6 hours·FormatGroup
